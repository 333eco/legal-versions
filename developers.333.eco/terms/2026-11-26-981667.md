## Each API's own rules

**Log in with HeartBank®.** Each person gets an ID that belongs to your app alone and cannot be linked to the same person in another app. You receive only the proof-of-personhood layers they choose to share, and no email, phone number, name or handle. Do not try to link a person across apps or to identify them from what you receive. Respect log-out: it ends your app's session and theirs. If you show the badge from our badge kit, use it as supplied, linked to our explainer; never show its absence, rank or gate people by it, animate it, or call it earned.

**Sign with HeartBank® (B-Origin℠).** A signature is a passkey press over a file's fingerprint, written to a public, append-only log. The file never leaves the person's device. A record shows that a passkey signed that fingerprint at that time. It does not show who made the file, that its content is true, or that it is lawful. The name of your tool goes into every record your app signs, permanently. Do not present a record as more than it is. A B-Trace℠ check through the API (with an API key) reads what a file carries; never show B-Signal℠ as a grade of someone's work, and show what the file carries instead.

**Confirm with HeartBank®.** One press for one named action, by a person your app already knows. Use it for the action you name, and nothing is kept.

**Thank with HeartBank®.** A thank is recorded on the gratitude ledger with that person's press. Kiitos and Kiitti have no cash value: they cannot be bought, sold, traded, redeemed or exchanged for money, goods or time, and no app may present them as money or a payment.
- Today only first-party apps write. Outside apps may write Kiitos from season 2, which begins 7 January 2027, after review.
- When the ledger declines a thank, it says only that nothing was written. It gives no reason, and that is by design. Do not try to infer one, and do not tell a person why.
- Your app receives only the entry's ID. The whole receipt goes to the two people, in their own records.
- A ring is drawn, never shown as a number. Do not rank, compare, count or display people by their rings.
- Do not stage, script or automate thanks.

**Look up with HeartBank®.** You receive exactly what a person's public page already shows. Show their name as they wrote it. Look up a name your user gives you; do not collect, list or index people's names in bulk.

**Verify with HeartBank®.** Free, with no key. Say plainly what a check does and does not show, as the docs do.
