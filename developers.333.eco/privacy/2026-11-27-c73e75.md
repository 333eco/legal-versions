# Privacy policy

Draft of 27 November 2026.

This policy covers developers.333.eco: its pages, its dashboard, its MCP server and its sandbox at sandbox.333.eco. It is run by Lucky Fate LLC. The people who use your apps are covered by the policies of the HeartBank® services they use, and by yours.

## What we keep about you as a developer

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| An ID that Log in with HeartBank® makes for this site alone, whether you used a passkey, when you logged in, and your language | to keep you logged in | a session lasts at most 12 hours |
| Each business's name and the contact email you give | to reach you about your apps | until you ask us to delete it |
| Each app's name, kind and website host; its Log-in settings (return addresses, your key address, log-out and change addresses, the layers it asks for); its review status and reason; when you accepted the developer terms, and which version (their date and a fingerprint of their text) | to run Log in and Sign for your app, and to review it | until you delete the app |
| For each API key: its name, its fingerprint, whether it is approved or revoked, and when you accepted the terms, and which version | to check your app's calls | until you delete the app |
| Each day's metered use, per key and per app | for your usage page, and for billing once it opens | while your business exists |
| A link to your balance at billing.thonly.com, as an opaque ID, and the monthly ceiling you set | to charge metered use once billing opens | until you ask us to remove it |
| Your sandbox test persons: the label you give each, and its app | to run the sandbox | until you remove them or the app |
| On each phone or computer where you turn on alerts: the address your browser's push service gave it, the keys to encrypt an alert to it, the language it was turned on in, and when | to tell you when a key is added to or removed from one of your apps, or an AI tool is connected to your apps | until you turn alerts off there or log out there, or the push service says the address is gone; at most 10 devices |
| When an AI tool you connected first works on your apps: a fingerprint of you, that tool and that connection together, never the tool's name or address, and when it was last seen | to tell you once per connection, not at every use | 15 days after it was last seen |

Every alert is also kept for the bell at the top of this site, whether or not you turned alerts on anywhere: what kind of alert it is, the time, and the page it opens, never which app, key or tool. Only you can read them; Clear all deletes them, and they are deleted after 90 days. When you open the bell, this site keeps the time, so the dot shows only what came after.

We never keep an API key itself, only its fingerprint, so nobody here can read it back. We never see a card or a bank account: payment details stay with billing.thonly.com and its payment processor, under that site's own policy.

When you ask for Log in or Sign to be reviewed, your app's name and website are sent to the reviewer by email. Your contact address is not.

Never put a real person's name or details in a sandbox test person's label.

## What the sandbox keeps about people testing the B-PoH℠ app

sandbox.333.eco also lets people test the B-PoH℠ app with their own phone before the app is released. It is a test server: a passkey or key made there works nowhere else, and a press made there is never a public record.

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| The passkey you make or use there: its public key, its ID, when it was added, and any name you give it | to let you add your phone | while a phone of yours is added; a passkey with no phone added is deleted each Monday |
| For each key the app makes on your phone: its public key, whether it is the finger key or the PIN key, what your phone's proof says about it (where the key is kept, whether the phone's software is unchanged and locked, its last security update, and until when the phone can prove new keys), a fingerprint of that proof and the serial numbers of the batch certificates in it (never the proof itself), a random ID the app makes for your phone, and which fingerprint, face or iris sensors your phone has | to add your phone and check its presses | until you ask us to remove it |
| Each test press: when it was made, which key made it, and what was signed (a file's fingerprint, its size and kind, and how it says it was made) | so the app can show you the press | until the Monday after it |

To remove your passkey and your phone's keys from the sandbox, write to us.

## What we keep about visitors

**Visit counts.** Each page tells the visit counter at thonly.org which page was opened, as its route only, never a record number or an ID; on a public page, the site a visitor came from (its address, not the page) and where a link led (our own sites by route, any other as "external"). With it go a random ID kept in your browser, your time zone, whether a robot drives the browser, and whether the visit is our own team's. That random ID is never linked to an account. There are no third-party scripts, advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

**Request logs.** Google Cloud, which runs this site, logs each request: its time, the address requested, the answer, your IP address and your browser's name. These logs are kept for 30 days.

**The MCP server.** A tool call is answered from what it sends. A check of a signed statement reads what it is given and stores none of it. Calls are logged like any request.

## Who else sees it

- Google Cloud hosts this site and its database, in Singapore.
- billing.thonly.com, once billing opens, receives your link and your metered use.
- The push service your browser uses (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft) carries each alert to your device. The alert is encrypted to your device, so the push service can't read its words.
- The reviewer reads your business and app details to decide a review.

We do not sell anything we keep, or use it for advertising.

## Your choices

You can delete an app, change your business's name and contact, and remove a test person in the dashboard. To delete a business or everything we keep about you, write to us. To see what we keep, write to us.
