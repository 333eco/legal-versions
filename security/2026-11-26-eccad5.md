# Security policy

Draft of 26 November 2026.

This policy covers every service whose documents are listed at legal.333.eco. It says how to report a weakness you find in one of them, what we promise if you look for weaknesses in good faith, and what we do with a report. Here, "we" means the operator of the service your report is about; the operators page at legal.333.eco/operators lists each one.

## How to report

Write to security@333.eco. Say which service and which address the weakness affects, what you found, and the steps that show it. Send it only to that address, never in public.

## Good-faith research

If you look for weaknesses in good faith and follow these rules, we will not take legal action against you, or ask anyone else to, for that research:

- **No harm to people's data.** Use only your own accounts and test data. If you reach anything that belongs to someone else, stop, keep none of it, and tell us.
- **No disruption.** No denial of service, no flooding, no spam, and no load that other people would notice.
- **No tricks on people.** No phishing or other social engineering, and no physical access to anyone's devices or offices.
- **Report privately.** Tell us at security@333.eco, and no one else, first.
- **Give us time to fix it.** Wait until we say it is fixed, or until we have agreed a date with you, before you tell anyone else.
- **Nothing in exchange.** Do not ask for payment or anything else in return for a report.

This promise covers only what we can promise. It cannot speak for other companies whose systems a service uses, and it does not change the law where you are.

## What we do with a report

We reply to say we have it, look into it, and fix what we find. We tell you when it is fixed, and we may ask you to try the fix. If you would like to be thanked by name when we say what was fixed, tell us; otherwise we keep your name to ourselves.

## No bounty

There is no reward for a report.

## Changes

Each change is published as its own version with its own date, at legal.333.eco, where every version stays.

**Contact:** security@333.eco
