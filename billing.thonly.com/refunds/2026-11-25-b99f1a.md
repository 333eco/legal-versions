# Refunds and cancellation

Draft of 25 November 2026.

This document is between you and Lucky Fate LLC, which sells the balance kept at billing.thonly.com, for now. It covers getting money back, stopping a monthly payment, a disputed payment, and what stays when you stop using this site. Today the site runs in test mode, and no real money moves.

## Your balance stays yours

- Your balance never expires.
- It never goes down by itself: only when it pays for something used through an app you allowed, when money goes back to you, or when a card payment is disputed (see below).
- Ask for the unspent part back at any time.
- It can't be sent to anyone.

## Getting the unspent part back

Log in and open Get it back (billing.thonly.com/back). It shows what will go back before anything moves. No reason is asked.

- It goes back the way you paid, newest payment first, and never more than what is left of each payment.
- A card payment goes back to the card that paid, through our payment company, Stripe. A card usually shows it within 5 to 10 business days.
- A payment made by KHQR is sent back by a person, to the account that paid. KHQR is offered only to people invited to try it.
- Whole cents only: an amount under a cent stays in your balance, and you can still spend it.
- Anything bought through Apple or Google is returned by them, not from this page.
- The money leaves your balance the moment you ask, so it can't be spent twice. If a card refund can't be sent, or fails on the way, the money comes back to your balance once, and we email you to arrange another way. If a KHQR refund can't be sent, the money comes back to your balance.
- If the payment company's answer is unclear, the money is not put back on a guess: it waits for the payment company's own answer, or for a person to settle it.

## A monthly payment, and how to stop it

A monthly payment is off until you set it, and only someone 18 or over can set one.

- You choose the amount, in whole dollars from $5 to $500, and a day from the 1st to the 28th. You read the exact words you agree to, then save your card on Stripe's own page.
- The first payment is on the next time that day comes, never the day you set it. Each payment adds to your balance, and we email a receipt each time.
- To stop it, press Stop on the Schedule page (billing.thonly.com/schedule). It stops at once, no reason is asked, and nothing more is charged. We ask Stripe to remove the saved card.
- Stopping changes nothing in your balance.
- Asking for money back stops it too: the Get it back page asks you to tick "Also stop adding money each month." first. The box starts empty.
- If your card is declined, that month is not tried again, and we email you once with the next date. After two declines in a row the monthly payment pauses, and we tell you once. You can set it again whenever you like.

## A disputed payment

If you dispute a card payment with the company that issued your card, the part of that payment still unspent comes out of your balance when the dispute is opened. If the dispute then closes without the company that issued your card taking the payment back, exactly that amount comes back to your balance, once. What was already spent is not asked back: your balance never goes below zero, and this site never records a debt.

## When you stop using this site

This site has no step that closes an account today. Nothing here ends a balance: logging out, disconnecting an app, stopping a monthly payment, or a parent taking back their consent for a teen leaves the balance as it is. To take your money out, ask for the unspent part back.
