# Privacy policy

Draft of 23 November 2026.

This policy covers B.333.eco: its links, their cards and B-QR codes, their about pages, and its public log. It is run by the organization that will be named in the final version. B-Links are made by HeartBank® apps, and each of those apps has its own policy.

## What a link keeps

When an app makes a B-Link, we keep: the address it opens, fixed when it is made and never changed; which app made it; its title and one sentence saying what it is, in English and, when the app gives one, in Khmer; the picture for its card, when there is one (kept in HeartBank®'s own store, and never shown by its storage address); the signed record it belongs to, when there is one; the @name or #tag the app chose to show as its maker or sharer, or nothing; whether its maker listed it in public; the day it was made; and the day it was ended, and by whom (its maker, or a takedown). These are kept for as long as the link exists. A link's code is never given to another link, so an ended link keeps its row and says that it was ended.

## What opening a link keeps

One number per link: how many times it was opened. Not when, not from where, not by whom, not on what device, and not from which page. Link-preview robots are not counted. Only the app that made a link can read its number, to show to the person who shared it, when they ask.

This site runs on Cloudflare. We have turned off its request logs for this site, so no log here keeps who opened a link. Cloudflare carries each request under its own policy.

## The public log

Every link is written once to the public log at B.333.eco/log, so anyone can check that a link was never pointed somewhere else. A link is written as two fingerprints, not as its address: if you hold a link you can find it, and nobody can read the log to find yours. Only a link its maker marked public appears in plain words. Nothing in the log is ever changed or removed.

## Visits to the front page

The front page tells the visit counter at thonly.org that it was opened, with a random ID kept in your browser, your time zone, and whether a robot drives the browser. That ID is never linked to an account. Links, cards, about pages and the log carry no counter. There are no third-party scripts, advertising or tracking cookies on this site. Your language and color choices are kept in your own browser.

## Who else sees it

- Cloudflare runs this site and its database.
- The Internet Archive keeps dated copies of the front page and the log, once a week.
- The app that made a link reads its own links and their counts.

We do not sell anything we keep, or use it for advertising.

## Your choices

The app that made a link lets its maker end it. To report a link, write to notice@333.eco with the link. To ask what we keep about a link you made, write to us.
