# Privacy policy

Draft of 26 November 2026.

The app collects nothing. This website counts visits, as described below. This page explains what that means, and — more usefully — what it does not mean.

sayyourname.app is published by Factory 333™, member of HeartBank®.

## The app

The app has no account system, no log-in and no server of ours. Names, recordings, photos and notes are written to the device that captured them and are not transmitted anywhere.

Because there is no account, there is also nothing to delete on our side, nobody to write to about a security issue, and no way for us to recover anything you lose. Those are real costs of the design and we accepted them on purpose.

## Recordings of other people

A voice recording is a biometric identifier under some state laws, including in Illinois, Texas and Washington. The recordings in this app are made by their own subject, in the moment, from your hands into theirs — which is the cleanest version of consent available — but the person holding the phone afterwards is holding somebody else’s voice.

> **Treat the export as what it is.** A backup file is a corpus of other people’s voices. It is encrypted with a passphrase you choose, it is not a share-sheet action, and it is meant for moving to a new phone — not for sending to anyone.

## On-device is not the same as private

The honest limit, stated plainly: this design stops *us* from being the leak. It does not stop the device from being one.

- Your phone is probably backed up to iCloud or Google.
- Someone who can unlock it can open the app.
- A device can be seized or forensically imaged at a border.

If any of those matter for the names you are keeping, keep fewer of them.

## This website

The site you are reading sets no cookies. It does count visits, in two ways.

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts the pages viewed and measures how fast they load.

A visit counter at thonly.org receives a note each time a page here is opened or a link is clicked. The note holds the page’s address, the page you came from, the address of the link you clicked, whether the site was opened from a home-screen icon, the tag a link carried naming where it was posted (if it had one), your time zone, whether the browser says it is being driven by software, and a random number this site keeps in your browser’s storage so that visits from the same browser can be told apart. The counter adds the time, the name of this site and your browser’s description of itself (its name, version and system). The record it keeps does not include your IP address. Each note may also reach the site’s founder as a notification on a phone. We have not yet set a limit on how long these notes are kept.

Neither one receives the names, recordings, photos or notes in the app. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, neither one runs.

Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which sees the request that fetches them.
