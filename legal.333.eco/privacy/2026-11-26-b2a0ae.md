# Privacy policy

Draft of 26 November 2026.

This policy covers legal.333.eco, the site that shows the legal documents of the services listed on it. It is run by the organization that will be named in the final version. **Contact:** miss.aquarius@heartbank.ceo

## What this site keeps about you

Nothing. The site runs no analytics script and sets no cookies. It has no accounts, no forms and no log-in, and its pages are the same for everyone.

## Who else handles data

Cloudflare serves this site. To deliver and protect each page, it handles each request, including the address it came from, under its own privacy policy. We see only Cloudflare's totals for the site, such as how many requests it answered, never a person.

## The documents themselves

Every version of every document here is also kept in the public repository 333eco/legal-versions, with its timestamps. Software Heritage and the Internet Archive are asked to keep copies. They hold the documents, never anything about you.

## Changes

Each change is published as its own version with its own date, at legal.333.eco, where every version stays.
