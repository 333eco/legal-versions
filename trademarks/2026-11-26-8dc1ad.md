## What you may do

- Use a mark's words to refer truthfully to the service or product it names, for example: "this app uses Log in with HeartBank®".
- Write each mark as it is written here, with its symbol at its first or most prominent use.

## What you may not do

- Suggest that we endorse, sponsor or partner with you, or that your product is ours, when it is not.
- Use a mark in your own product's name, company name or domain name.
- Change the B-Emblem™: do not redraw, stretch, crop or add to it, or combine it with another mark. Use it only as supplied.
- Use a mark in a way that is false or misleading, or that attacks anyone.

## The badge kit

Show the badge kit's B-Passkey℠ Verified badge only as the developer terms allow: as supplied, linked to our explainer. Never show its absence, rank or gate people by it, animate it, or call it earned. It says that a passkey was used, never who someone is.
